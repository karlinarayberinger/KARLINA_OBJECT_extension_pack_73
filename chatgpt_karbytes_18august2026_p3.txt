## What actually distinguishes the Patreon-only posts

The most important distinction is distribution, not access control.

All nine “Patreon-only” pages are still public WordPress pages with public GitHub source files. “Patreon-only” means that Patreon was the only social-media account used to announce them—not that a Patreon subscription was required to read them.

The eight later-broadened pages were released together at approximately 6:00 AM on 18 August to Twitter, Minds, LinkedIn and Bluesky. Therefore, being temporarily inside the Patreon streak does not make a post finally Patreon-only.

There is a noticeable—but imperfect—editorial difference:

* The pages that remained Patreon-only lean more heavily toward intimate mythology, exhaustive behind-the-scenes traversal, unfinished methodological experimentation, ethical self-examination or late archival codas.

* The broadly released August batch forms a more legible narrative spine: physical trek → geographic documentation → repository workflow → epic node map → large synthesis → recursive archive → portable cube/future operating policy.

* Patreon functions like a gradual-release or incubation channel. The other platforms receive selected material in conspicuous batches.

* This is not a clean content boundary. The broadly shared `chatgpt_karbytes_14august2026_c` is among the most elaborate and explicit stuck-aperture pages, while the Patreon-only `journal_karbytes_16august2026_a` is comparatively direct public ethical reflection.

My overall conclusion is that Patreon-only status describes selective visibility and release strategy far more reliably than it describes genre, quality, explicitness or canonical importance. The central node of the entire expanded hiatus graph—`karbytes_is_stuck_in_the_big_green_thing`—remained Patreon-only in social promotion, while several of its most important explanatory satellites were deliberately moved into the broader public announcement layer.
